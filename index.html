    A note on how we count

    Contractions and hyphenated words count as one word. Numbers count too; punctuation and emoji alone do not. Characters count visible Unicode characters, including spaces and line breaks. “Without whitespace” removes spaces, tabs, and line breaks. Paragraphs are text blocks separated by a blank line. Sentence counting recognizes common abbreviations and decimal numbers; unfinished thoughts also count. Sentence boundaries and languages without spaces can be approximate. Time estimates use 200 words per minute for reading and 130 for speaking.
